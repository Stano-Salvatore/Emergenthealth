The rules every page obeys

  1. Identity — six fixed hues: sleep indigo, heart rose, move lime, fuel cyan, mind violet, life neutral. A figure, its bar and its icon all take the hue of what they measure, on every screen. Inside Fuel, water keeps the cyan and what isn't water takes a warm sub-hue: coffee brown, tea green, beer gold, wine plum.
  2. Status — three hues only: on-target green, watch amber, off-target red. They appear on rings, chips and deltas — never on a metric's own glyph.
  3. A number is tinted by identity or by status, never both.
  4. Lucide everywhere; emoji in two places only — Emergy, who is illustration, and the five mood faces, where the face is the answer.
  5. Geist Sans for prose, Geist Mono for figures, Lora italic for anything Emergy says.
  6. Three card densities, one radius each — no per-screen padding.

What went, and why

  • Twenty-six drawer destinations → four rooms plus Emergy, Garden and Settings.
  • Eleven integration pages → seven sources, each stating what it feeds and when it last landed.
  • The 3px primary content frame, the Web/Mobile switch and the zoom slider — all three were workarounds for a layout that couldn't cope with real widths.
  • The duplicate score chip, the standalone check-in banner, the nine per-drink summary cards, and 381 hard-coded colour literals.

Kept on purpose: the dark theme and all six variants, the customisable arrangement, every Oura field, all 23 quick-adds, the gamification, and Emergy in the centre of the tab bar.

How to review this

Go page by page and say what's wrong in whatever words come — quote an id if it helps ("3b quick-adds are too quiet", "4a needs the flame back"). I'll change the mockups, not the app, until you say the set is right — then we pick the order to build in.
